MetroMove Transit EDA

Tableau — an exploratory dashboard for MetroMove Transit Solutions, a fictional transport operator.

Tools & Skills

  • Tableau dashboards
  • Calculated fields
  • Parameter-driven filtering

Approach

Built linked views of ridership, on-time performance, and route load, with parameters letting users switch between daily and weekly granularity.

Outcome

Made it easy to spot which routes and time bands were most prone to delays.
